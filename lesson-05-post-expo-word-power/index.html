Lesson 05: Post-Expo Word Power

You are the Logistics Coordinator after the International Expo. Upgrade basic language into precise professional English using word families, then negotiate clearer contract terms.

Word families Professional tone Contract language TOEIC Text Completion

Scenario

Role: Logistics Coordinator for GlobalTech after the International Expo.

Challenge: Mr. Henderson (CEO) says your updates sound "like a student wrote them." Legal also needs clearer contract language with the vendor.

Goal: Choose the correct word family form (noun, verb, adjective, adverb) so your emails and negotiations sound professional and precise.

Grammar Reference · Word Families

In Business English and TOEIC, the missing word is often a different form of the same family. Match the form to the grammar slot.

Noun slotsAfter the / a / our / company’s
The company’s decision…
Adjective slotsBefore a noun or after be / seem
A more flexible arrangement
Adverb slotsModify a verb or adjective
worked efficiently · completely satisfied

Core families for this lesson

decide → decision · decisive · decisively

negotiate → negotiation · negotiable

deliver → delivery · deliverable

satisfy → satisfaction · satisfied / satisfactory · satisfactorily

flexible → flexibility

cancel → cancellation · cancelled

fail → failure · failed

complete → completion · completed · completely

Connection with previous lessons

-ed / -ing (L03): The team was satisfied with the satisfying results.

Simple Present (L04): The company operates in ten countries. Its operations are global.

First Conditional: If we negotiate well, we will reach an agreement.

Indirect question: Could you tell me if the delivery is guaranteed?

Common mistakes

Not: The ship was late. → Correct: The shipment was late.

Not: The equip was good. → Correct: The equipment performed well.

Not: We need to make a decide. → Correct: We need to make a decision.

Not: The team worked efficient. → Correct: The team worked efficiently.

Corrected / upgraded version

Subject: Post-Expo Assessment – International Expo

Dear Mr. Henderson,

I am writing to provide an assessment of the Expo. We made an early decision to organize all operations in advance. The shipment of German equipment was delayed; however, we found a solution. The team was satisfied with the on-time completion of tasks.

I recommend that we reach an agreement on improved regulations for future events. The booth materials performed well. Please confirm your satisfaction with my performance. If the vendor fails again, we will reopen the negotiation.

Best regards,
Logistics Coordinator

Listening · The Contract Call

Script (teacher / self-study)

“Good afternoon. This is Patricia Hale from Legal. I’m calling regarding the vendor agreement for next year’s Expo. We have reviewed the terms, and we have some concerns about the cancellation clause. The vendor’s proposal lacks flexibility. We need a more flexible arrangement. Specifically, we require the option to terminate the contract with 30 days’ notice. Additionally, the liability section is insufficient. We cannot accept unlimited liability for delays caused by third-party shippers. Please prepare a revised version and send it to our office by Wednesday. We look forward to your response.”

Comprehension Listening Questions

Choose the best answer from the call (word family / meaning).

1. What does Patricia think the vendor’s proposal lacks?
2. What kind of arrangement do they need?
3. How does she describe the liability section?
4. What should the vendor prepare?
