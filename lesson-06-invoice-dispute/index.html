Lesson 06: Resolving an Invoice Dispute

You work in Accounts Payable. A supplier invoice looks wrong. Use prefixes and suffixes to read, discuss, and write precise finance language.

Prefixes & suffixes Finance English Invoice language TOEIC word forms

Scenario

Role: Accounts Payable Officer at Northline Retail.

Challenge: Invoice #NR-4821 from supplier BrightPack Co. may include an overcharge. Your manager wants a clear explanation and a professional email to Accounts Receivable.

Goal: Use common business prefixes and suffixes to form precise finance words (not just “basic” vocabulary).

Grammar Reference · Prefixes & Suffixes

Affixes change meaning or word class. In finance emails and TOEIC, the gap often needs the built form, not the base word.

Prefixes = meaningun- not · re- again · over- too much · under- too little · pre- before · non- not · mis- wrongly · dis- opposite
Suffixes = word class-tion / -ment → noun
-able / -ible → adjective
-ly → adverb
Finance examplespay → payable / payment / prepayment
refund → refundable / non-refundable
clear → clarification

Prefix map (this lesson)

over- overcharge · overpayment

under- undercharge · underpayment

pre- prepayment · pre-approved

re- reissue · recalculate · resend

un- unavailable · unpaid

non- non-refundable · non-negotiable

mis- mislabeled · miscalculated

dis- disagreement · disconnect (rare in AP; know the pattern)

Suffix map (this lesson)

-tion / -sion clarification · transaction · discussion

-ment payment · shipment · agreement

-able / -ible payable · refundable · eligible

-ity availability · responsibility

-ly correctly · immediately · currently

Common mistakes

Not: Please send a clarify. → Correct: Please send a clarification.

Not: The fee is not refund. → Correct: The fee is non-refundable.

Not: We need reissue the invoice. → Correct: We need to reissue / Please reissue the invoice.

Not: There was an overpay on the card. → Correct: There was an overpayment.

Reading · Supplier Note on Invoice NR-4821

Read the short note from BrightPack Accounts Receivable. Then complete the text-completion items (same skill family as TOEIC Part 6).

BRIGHTPACK CO. · Accounts Receivable

Subject: Re: Invoice NR-4821 — packaging & freight

Dear Northline AP Team,

Thank you for your message regarding invoice NR-4821. We have reviewed the file. The original packing list was briefly unavailable in our system, which caused a delay in our response.

Regarding line 3, we agree there may be a pricing error. If our team miscalculated the packaging rate, we will reissue the invoice within two business days. Any confirmed overpayment will be credited to your account; standard deposits remain non-refundable only when the order is cancelled after production starts.

Please confirm the balance payable you currently show in your system so we can close this disagreement quickly.

Best regards,
Elena Ruiz
Accounts Receivable · BrightPack Co.

Script (teacher / self-study)

“Hi, this is Elena from BrightPack Accounts Receivable. I’m calling about invoice NR-4821. We checked the packaging rate again. There was a miscalculation on line 3, so we will reissue the invoice today. Your prepayment is already in the system. After the credit, the balance payable should be one thousand one hundred sixty dollars. The rush-order deposit is non-refundable, but the overcharge will not stay on the account. Could you confirm the balance payable once you receive the revised invoice? Thanks.”
