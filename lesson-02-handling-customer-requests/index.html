Lesson 02: Handling Customer Requests Professionally

You work at the information desk of an international logistics expo. Visitors need clear information about appointments, meeting rooms, departments, and service problems.

Trade fair receptionCustomer serviceIndirect questionsProfessional tone

Scenario

Role: Information desk agent at an international logistics expo.

Challenge: Help visitors without sounding too direct or impatient.

Goal: Use indirect questions to request and provide information professionally.

Visitor opening line

“Hi, I have an appointment with the export team, but I cannot find the right meeting room.”

10 minWarm-up: direct vs. professional questions.
15 minVocabulary cards and meaning check.
15 minIndirect-question grammar practice.
10 minListening: information desk problem.
10 minRole play: visitors with different needs.
HomeworkRecord a two-minute call requesting information.
Practice Vocabulary in Context
1. The visitor needs a printed ID before entering the expo.
2. The company showing its products is an...
3. A reserved meeting period is a...
4. If you help someone find a location, you...

Indirect Questions

Use indirect questions when you need information but want to sound professional, patient, and respectful.

Direct: Where is the meeting room?

Indirect: Could you tell me where the meeting room is?

Word Order

After the polite opener, use statement word order.

Correct: Do you know where the export team is?

Not: Do you know where is the export team?

Speaking Frame

Could you tell me...? Do you know...? Would you mind telling me...? May I ask...?

Transform each direct question into an indirect question. Use a different opener for each one.

Where is booth 12?
Who is the regional manager?
When does the meeting start?
Is the supplier available?

Read the dialogue. Identify the indirect questions that sound incorrect, then rewrite them with statement word order.

Agent: Of course. Could you tell me where is your appointment?

Visitor: I am meeting with the regional manager.

Teacher Check

Expected corrections: Could you tell me where your appointment is? / Would you mind telling me what company you represent?

Listening Task

Listen to the conversation at an international logistics expo. A visitor needs help finding a meeting and asks for extra event information.

Visitor: Good morning. I'm here for a meeting with the export team, but I'm not sure where to go.

Agent: Good morning. Welcome to the GlobalTech Logistics information desk. Could you tell me who you are meeting with?

Visitor: I'm meeting with Ana Torres. I think the meeting is at 11:30, but my badge only says Hall B.

Agent: Thank you. Let me check that for you. Do you know where is your confirmation email?

Visitor: Yes, I have it on my phone. One moment... Here it is.

Agent: Great, thank you. I can see your appointment with Ana Torres. It says Conference Room B4. Would you mind telling me what company do you represent?

Visitor: Of course. I'm from Northbridge Supplies.

Agent: Thank you. Your meeting is in Conference Room B4. Go straight ahead, turn left after booth 18, and the room is next to the registration desk.

Visitor: Thank you. Also, do you know when does the supplier presentation start?

Agent: The supplier presentation starts at 12:15 in Hall C.

Visitor: Great. And could you tell me where is the networking session?

Agent: Yes. The networking session is in the main venue, next to the coffee area. You'll see a sign that says "Networking Lounge."

Visitor: Perfect. I'll go to Conference Room B4 first, and then I'll attend the presentation.

Agent: That sounds good. If you need anything else, please come back to the information desk.

Visitor: Thank you for your help.

Agent: You're welcome. Enjoy the expo.

Comprehension Listening Questions

Choose the best answer. Some options are close, so listen for the reason, sequence, and purpose.

1. Why does the visitor stop at the information desk?
2. What helps the agent confirm the correct meeting room?
3. Which sequence best matches what the visitor plans to do?
4. What can you infer about the agent's main role in the conversation?
5. Which detail is true about the networking session?

Speaking Role Play Scenario

You work at the GlobalTech Logistics information desk. A visitor has a meeting at the expo, but their badge and email do not give them enough clear information. Help them confirm the appointment, find the correct room, and ask about another event using indirect questions.

Appointment unclear
Badge only says Hall B
Extra event question

Visitor opening line

"Good morning. I'm here for a meeting with the export team, but I'm not sure where to go."

Student Goal

  • Greet the visitor professionally.
  • Ask at least four indirect questions.
  • Confirm the meeting room and give clear directions.
  • Answer one extra question about the expo schedule.
